About

Our Mission

The Centre for Sanskrit Learning (CSL) at IIT Bombay is dedicated to advancing the study of Sanskrit — not merely as a classical language, but as a living repository of scientific, philosophical, and literary knowledge.

We collaborate with institutions such as Vidyasvam and Kavikulaguru Kalidas Sanskrit University to create a bridge between traditional scholarship and modern computational tools.

Research & Resources

Tools & Projects

Sanskrit Wordnet

A large lexical database of Sanskrit, developed at IIT Bombay, linking synsets across Hindi, English, and other languages.

Shobhika Font

A Devanagari typeface created under the SandHI initiative at IIT Bombay, designed for scholarship and digital publication.

Digitization Efforts

Ongoing work on digitizing manuscripts and scientific literature in Sanskrit, with structured metadata and search support.
